Our story

A small bakery that bakes everything from scratch, every day, for the moments people remember.

Started with one oven and a lot of birthdays

I Cake began in a home kitchen, baking for friends and family. Word spread one birthday at a time, and today we bake for weddings, school parties, office celebrations and quiet midweek cravings.

We still work the same way: real butter and cream, fresh fruit, proper chocolate, and no shortcuts on the things you taste.
